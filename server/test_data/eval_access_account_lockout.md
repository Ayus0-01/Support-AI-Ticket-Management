# Account Lockout Troubleshooting

## Overview

This article provides troubleshooting guidance for company accounts that have been locked or blocked after repeated unsuccessful authentication attempts.

## Symptoms

Common symptoms include:

- Account locked messages
- Sign-in attempts being blocked
- The user cannot authenticate to the company account
- The account repeatedly becomes locked
- Authentication remains unavailable after multiple failed attempts

## Troubleshooting Steps

### 1. Confirm the lockout condition

Confirm that the authentication message indicates that the account is locked or blocked.

Do not assume that every login failure is an account lockout.

### 2. Stop repeated login attempts

Stop entering credentials repeatedly.

Repeated unsuccessful attempts may recreate or extend the lockout condition.

### 3. Confirm the affected account

Verify that the user is attempting to sign in to the correct corporate account.

### 4. Consider other active sessions

Determine whether another workstation, mobile device, or application may still be attempting authentication with outdated credentials.

### 5. Follow the approved recovery process

Use the organization's approved account-unlock or account-recovery process.

Do not bypass account security controls.

### 6. Escalate unresolved lockouts

Escalate when:

- The account remains locked
- Administrative unlocking is required
- The account repeatedly becomes locked
- The source of repeated failed authentication attempts is unknown
- The user cannot complete the approved recovery process

## Important Safety Notes

Do not bypass account protection.

Do not disable lockout controls.

Do not unlock another user's account without authorization.

## Resolution Outcome

The expected outcome is restoration of account access through the organization's approved account-unlock or recovery process.

## Escalation

Escalation is recommended when administrator intervention is required or repeated lockouts cannot be explained.
