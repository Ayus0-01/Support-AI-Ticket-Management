# Laptop Hardware Troubleshooting

## Overview

This article provides troubleshooting guidance for hardware problems affecting company laptops.

Use this guidance when the physical laptop device is malfunctioning, not behaving normally, or showing signs of a hardware-related problem.

## Symptoms

Common symptoms include:

- Laptop hardware is not responding normally
- The laptop does not behave as expected during normal operation
- A physical laptop component appears to be malfunctioning
- The laptop experiences a hardware-related fault
- The device requires hardware-level investigation

## Troubleshooting Steps

### 1. Identify the affected laptop

Confirm the company laptop experiencing the hardware problem.

Record the specific component or observable symptom when possible.

### 2. Check obvious physical connections

Check accessible power and external connections.

Do not open the laptop chassis or perform unauthorized hardware repairs.

### 3. Restart the laptop

Restart the laptop and determine whether the hardware problem persists.

### 4. Check whether the problem is repeatable

Repeat the affected operation once when safe.

Record whether the same hardware symptom occurs consistently.

### 5. Check for visible physical damage

Inspect the device for obvious physical damage such as a damaged connector, cracked enclosure, or other visible hardware issue.

Do not attempt physical repair.

### 6. Record useful diagnostic information

Record:

- Laptop identifier when available
- Affected component
- Observable symptoms
- Whether the problem is intermittent or continuous
- Whether the issue began after a known physical event

### 7. Escalate persistent hardware problems

Escalate when:

- The hardware problem persists
- Physical repair or replacement may be required
- The device has visible physical damage
- The affected component cannot be safely identified
- Further hardware diagnostics are required

## Important Safety Notes

Do not open the laptop chassis unless authorized.

Do not attempt unauthorized hardware repair.

Do not bypass device security controls.

## Resolution Outcome

The expected outcome is restoration of normal laptop operation or escalation for hardware diagnosis, repair, or replacement.

## Escalation

Escalation is recommended when hardware intervention or replacement is required.
