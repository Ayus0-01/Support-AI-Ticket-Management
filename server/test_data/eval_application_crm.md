# CRM Application Troubleshooting

## Overview

This article provides troubleshooting guidance for issues affecting approved customer relationship management applications.

## Symptoms

Common symptoms include:

- CRM application does not load correctly
- Customer records cannot be opened
- A CRM function reports an error
- A business action cannot be completed
- The CRM application behaves unexpectedly

## Troubleshooting Steps

### 1. Identify the affected CRM function

Record the CRM application, screen, record type, or business function that is affected.

### 2. Record the exact error

Capture the exact error message or observable failure.

### 3. Retry the affected operation

Repeat the operation once to determine whether the problem is consistent.

### 4. Check whether other CRM functions work

Determine whether the issue affects one function or the wider CRM application.

### 5. Restart the application

Close and reopen the CRM application and retry the affected operation.

### 6. Determine the scope

Identify whether the issue affects only one user or multiple users.

### 7. Escalate persistent CRM issues

Escalate when the problem persists or application-level investigation is required.

## Important Safety Notes

Do not modify customer records as a troubleshooting shortcut.

Do not repeatedly submit business transactions when doing so could create duplicate records.

## Resolution Outcome

The expected outcome is restoration of the affected CRM function or escalation with appropriate diagnostic information.

## Escalation

Escalate persistent or business-critical CRM issues to the appropriate application-support team.
