# Email Mailbox Access and Synchronization Troubleshooting

## Symptoms

Use this guide when a user cannot open the mailbox or when new
messages are not appearing on the user's device.

## Check Service Connectivity

Confirm that the device has working network connectivity.

Verify whether the organization's email service is reachable
from the approved webmail or browser interface.

## Test the Mailbox Through Webmail

Sign in to the approved webmail service using the user's current
corporate credentials.

Check whether recent messages are visible there.

If webmail works while the desktop or mobile client does not,
the problem is likely limited to the client or its synchronization
state.

## Refresh Client Synchronization

Close and reopen the email client.

Allow the client time to synchronize the mailbox again.

Confirm that the client is configured with the user's correct
corporate account.

## Check Mailbox Capacity

Verify whether the mailbox has reached its permitted storage
quota.

If the mailbox is full, follow the organization's approved
mailbox-cleanup or quota-remediation process.

## Escalation

Escalate when the mailbox cannot be accessed through webmail,
the service reports an account-side error, or synchronization
continues to fail after the client has been restarted.

Include the mailbox identifier, exact error message, and whether
webmail access succeeds.
