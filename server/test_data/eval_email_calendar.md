# Email Calendar Troubleshooting

## Overview

This article provides troubleshooting guidance for problems affecting the corporate email calendar, including calendar access, synchronization, and calendar-related functionality.

## Symptoms

Common symptoms include:

- Calendar does not open correctly
- Calendar events do not synchronize
- New events are not appearing
- Existing calendar information is missing or outdated
- Calendar actions fail unexpectedly

## Troubleshooting Steps

### 1. Identify the affected calendar function

Confirm whether the problem involves opening the calendar, viewing events, creating events, or synchronization.

### 2. Confirm the affected account

Verify that the user is signed in with the intended corporate email account.

### 3. Restart the email application

Close the corporate email application completely and reopen it.

Retry the affected calendar operation.

### 4. Check calendar synchronization

Determine whether recent calendar changes appear correctly across the approved email or calendar clients.

### 5. Test another calendar action

Try opening an existing event or creating a non-critical test event when appropriate.

Determine whether the problem affects the entire calendar or a specific action.

### 6. Record the observed error

Capture the exact error message or unusual calendar behavior.

### 7. Escalate persistent calendar problems

Escalate when:

- Calendar synchronization continues to fail
- Calendar data is missing unexpectedly
- Calendar access remains unavailable
- Administrative or service-level investigation is required

## Important Safety Notes

Do not delete or modify important calendar events as a troubleshooting shortcut.

Do not repeatedly create duplicate events while testing.

## Resolution Outcome

The expected outcome is restoration of normal calendar access and synchronization.

## Escalation

Escalate persistent calendar or synchronization issues to the appropriate email-support team.
