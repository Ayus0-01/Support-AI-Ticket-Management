# Desktop Hardware Troubleshooting

## Overview

This article provides troubleshooting guidance for hardware problems affecting company desktop computers.

## Symptoms

Common symptoms include:

- Desktop hardware is not responding normally
- A physical desktop component is malfunctioning
- The workstation does not operate normally because of a suspected hardware issue
- A connected component appears to have failed

## Troubleshooting Steps

### 1. Identify the affected desktop

Confirm the company desktop or workstation experiencing the hardware problem.

Record the affected component when possible.

### 2. Check accessible connections

Verify accessible power and external cable connections.

Do not open the computer case or perform unauthorized repairs.

### 3. Restart the workstation

Restart the desktop and check whether the hardware issue persists.

### 4. Check whether the issue is repeatable

Repeat the affected operation once when safe and note whether the problem occurs again.

### 5. Check for visible physical damage

Inspect accessible areas for obvious physical damage.

Do not attempt physical repair.

### 6. Record diagnostic information

Record:

- Workstation identifier when available
- Affected component
- Observable symptoms
- Whether the issue is intermittent or continuous
- Any recent physical event associated with the problem

### 7. Escalate persistent problems

Escalate when hardware diagnosis, repair, or replacement is required.

## Important Safety Notes

Do not open the desktop case without authorization.

Do not perform unauthorized hardware repairs.

## Resolution Outcome

The expected outcome is restoration of normal workstation operation or escalation for hardware service.

## Escalation

Escalate persistent or physical hardware problems to the appropriate hardware-support team.
