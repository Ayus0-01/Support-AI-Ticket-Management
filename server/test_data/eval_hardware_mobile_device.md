# Mobile Device Hardware Troubleshooting

## Overview

This article provides troubleshooting guidance for company-managed mobile devices experiencing hardware-related problems.

## Symptoms

Common symptoms include:

- Company mobile device is not responding normally
- Device hardware is malfunctioning
- Physical buttons or components are not functioning
- Device cannot operate normally because of a suspected hardware problem

## Troubleshooting Steps

### 1. Identify the affected mobile device

Confirm the company-managed mobile device experiencing the problem.

### 2. Record the affected component

Identify the specific hardware component or observable symptom when possible.

### 3. Restart the device

Restart the mobile device and check whether the hardware problem persists.

### 4. Check for visible damage

Inspect the device for obvious physical damage.

Do not attempt to physically repair the device.

### 5. Check whether the problem is repeatable

Repeat the affected operation once when safe and record whether the issue consistently occurs.

### 6. Record useful information

Document:

- Device identifier when available
- Affected component
- Observable symptoms
- Whether the problem is intermittent or continuous
- Any known physical event associated with the problem

### 7. Escalate persistent hardware problems

Escalate when repair, replacement, or additional hardware diagnostics may be required.

## Important Safety Notes

Do not disassemble the device.

Do not attempt unauthorized hardware repair.

Do not bypass device-management or security controls.

## Resolution Outcome

The expected outcome is restoration of normal device operation or escalation for approved repair or replacement.

## Escalation

Escalate persistent hardware faults to the appropriate mobile-device or hardware-support team.
