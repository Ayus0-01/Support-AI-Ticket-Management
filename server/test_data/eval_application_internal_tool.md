# Internal Tool Troubleshooting

## Overview

This article provides troubleshooting guidance for approved internal business tools developed or maintained for organizational use.

## Symptoms

Common symptoms include:

- Internal tool does not open correctly
- A business function does not work
- A screen reports an unexpected error
- A workflow cannot be completed
- The internal tool behaves differently from normal operation

## Troubleshooting Steps

### 1. Identify the internal tool

Record the exact tool, function, screen, or workflow affected.

### 2. Capture the observed error

Record the exact message or behavior shown by the application.

### 3. Retry the affected action

Repeat the affected action once and determine whether the problem is consistent.

### 4. Check whether other functions work

Determine whether the problem affects one function or the entire internal tool.

### 5. Restart the tool

Close and reopen the internal tool, then retry the affected operation.

### 6. Determine the scope

Check whether the problem affects only the requester or multiple users.

### 7. Escalate persistent issues

Escalate when the problem remains unresolved or requires application-specific investigation.

## Important Safety Notes

Do not modify production data or application configuration without authorization.

Do not bypass application security controls.

## Resolution Outcome

The expected outcome is restoration of the affected internal business function or escalation with useful diagnostic information.

## Escalation

Escalate persistent issues to the appropriate internal-tool support or application owner.
