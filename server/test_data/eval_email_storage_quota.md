# Email Storage Quota Troubleshooting

## Overview

This article provides troubleshooting guidance for corporate mailboxes that are approaching or exceeding their assigned storage quota.

## Symptoms

Common symptoms include:

- Mailbox quota warnings
- New messages cannot be received
- Email sending or receiving is affected by storage limits
- The mailbox reports insufficient storage
- The user cannot store additional email content

## Troubleshooting Steps

### 1. Confirm the quota warning

Verify that the email system reports a mailbox storage or quota-related condition.

### 2. Review mailbox usage

Identify whether the mailbox contains large messages, attachments, or accumulated content contributing to storage usage.

### 3. Review unnecessary mailbox content

Identify messages or other mailbox content that can be removed according to the organization's retention and data-handling requirements.

### 4. Remove content only when authorized

Delete unnecessary content only when doing so is permitted by organizational policy and retention requirements.

### 5. Recheck mailbox capacity

After permitted cleanup, verify whether available mailbox capacity has improved.

### 6. Avoid unsafe cleanup

Do not delete business-critical or retention-protected information merely to reduce mailbox size.

### 7. Escalate persistent quota problems

Escalate when:

- The mailbox remains over quota after permitted cleanup
- Storage limits cannot be changed at the user level
- Retention requirements prevent cleanup
- Administrative intervention is required

## Important Safety Notes

Do not delete messages solely to resolve a quota warning when the messages may be required for business, legal, compliance, or retention purposes.

Follow organizational retention policies before removing mailbox content.

## Resolution Outcome

The expected outcome is restoration of sufficient mailbox capacity through approved cleanup or administrative quota management.

## Escalation

Escalate when mailbox capacity requires administrative changes or retention rules prevent safe cleanup.
