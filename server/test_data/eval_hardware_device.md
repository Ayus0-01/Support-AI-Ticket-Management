# Hardware and Device Troubleshooting

## Symptoms

Use this guide when a laptop, desktop, monitor, keyboard, mouse,
or other physical computer component is not working correctly.

## Check Power and Connections

Confirm that the affected device is powered on and that its
power cable is securely connected.

For externally connected devices, verify that the cable is firmly
connected at both ends.

## Restart the Device

Save any available work and restart the affected computer.

After the restart, test the hardware again before performing
additional troubleshooting.

## Check the Physical Device

Inspect the affected hardware for visible damage, loose cables,
or unusual indicator lights.

Do not open or disassemble company hardware unless the organization's
approved support procedure specifically permits it.

## Test an Approved Alternative

When appropriate, connect a known-working compatible peripheral
or use an approved alternative port.

If the alternative works, record which component or connection
appears to be affected.

## Check Device Recognition

Verify whether the operating system recognizes the affected
hardware.

Record any device error, warning, or hardware-related message
shown by the operating system.

## Escalation

Escalate when the hardware remains unavailable after power,
connection, restart, and approved alternative-device checks.

Include the device name or asset tag, symptoms, error message,
and any visible hardware indicators.
