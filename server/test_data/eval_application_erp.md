# ERP Application Troubleshooting

## Overview

This article provides troubleshooting guidance for issues affecting approved enterprise resource planning applications.

ERP issues may involve unavailable business functions, errors while performing ERP operations, or unexpected application behavior.

## Symptoms

Common symptoms include:

- ERP application does not load correctly
- A business function fails
- An ERP screen reports an error
- A transaction cannot be completed
- The ERP application behaves differently from normal operation

## Troubleshooting Steps

### 1. Identify the affected ERP function

Record the exact ERP application, module, screen, or business function that is affected.

### 2. Capture the error

Record the exact error message or observable behavior.

### 3. Confirm whether the issue is repeatable

Repeat the affected operation once and determine whether the same problem occurs.

### 4. Check whether other ERP functions work

Determine whether the problem is isolated to one function or affects the broader ERP application.

### 5. Restart the application

Close and reopen the ERP application, then retry the affected operation.

### 6. Record the business impact

Identify whether the issue affects only the requester, a team, or a wider business function.

### 7. Escalate persistent ERP issues

Escalate when the issue persists after normal troubleshooting or when application-level investigation is required.

## Important Safety Notes

Do not modify production ERP data as a troubleshooting action.

Do not retry transactions repeatedly when doing so could create duplicate business records.

## Resolution Outcome

The expected outcome is restoration of the affected ERP function or escalation with sufficient diagnostic information.

## Escalation

Escalate persistent or business-critical ERP problems to the appropriate application-support team.
