# Docking Station Troubleshooting

## Overview

This article provides troubleshooting guidance for company docking stations that are not functioning correctly with an approved workstation.

## Symptoms

Common symptoms include:

- Docking station is not detected
- External monitors connected through the dock are unavailable
- USB devices connected through the dock do not work
- Laptop does not properly connect through the docking station
- Docking station functions intermittently

## Troubleshooting Steps

### 1. Confirm the affected docking station

Identify the docking station and the company laptop or workstation connected to it.

### 2. Check physical connections

Verify that the docking station has the expected power and that its connection to the workstation is secure.

### 3. Reconnect the docking station

Disconnect the dock from the workstation and reconnect it securely.

### 4. Restart the workstation

Restart the connected laptop or workstation and reconnect the docking station.

### 5. Test the affected peripheral

Determine whether the issue affects one connected device or multiple devices connected through the dock.

### 6. Check whether the dock is consistently detected

Reconnect the dock and determine whether it is recognized consistently by the workstation.

### 7. Escalate persistent docking problems

Escalate when:

- The docking station remains undetected
- Multiple connected peripherals remain unavailable
- Physical replacement may be required
- Further hardware diagnostics are necessary

## Important Safety Notes

Do not modify hardware firmware or configuration unless explicitly authorized.

Do not attempt physical repairs.

## Resolution Outcome

The expected outcome is normal docking-station operation and connectivity to the approved workstation and attached peripherals.

## Escalation

Escalate persistent docking-station failures to the appropriate hardware-support team.
