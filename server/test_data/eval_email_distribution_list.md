# Email Distribution List Troubleshooting

## Overview

This article provides troubleshooting guidance for corporate email distribution lists, including problems sending to lists, receiving messages from lists, or accessing approved distribution-list addresses.

## Symptoms

Common symptoms include:

- Messages sent to a distribution list are not delivered
- The user cannot send to a distribution list
- Expected messages from a distribution list are not received
- A distribution list address is rejected
- Distribution-list membership appears incorrect

## Troubleshooting Steps

### 1. Identify the affected distribution list

Record the distribution-list address and the operation that is failing.

### 2. Confirm the recipient address

Verify that the correct approved distribution-list address is being used.

### 3. Check whether ordinary email works

Determine whether the user's mailbox can send and receive normal messages successfully.

### 4. Retry the message when safe

Retry sending the message once using the verified distribution-list address.

### 5. Record the delivery result

Capture any delivery failure or rejection message returned by the email system.

### 6. Determine whether membership is relevant

If the user expects to receive messages from the list, confirm whether the user is expected to be a member of that distribution list.

### 7. Follow the approved distribution-list process

If membership or list configuration needs to change, follow the organization's approved process.

### 8. Escalate persistent list issues

Escalate when:

- Messages continue to be rejected
- Membership appears incorrect
- The distribution list may be misconfigured
- Administrative changes are required
- The delivery failure cannot be explained

## Important Safety Notes

Do not modify distribution-list membership without authorization.

Do not repeatedly send sensitive information while testing delivery.

## Resolution Outcome

The expected outcome is successful distribution-list communication or completion of the approved membership/configuration process.

## Escalation

Escalate persistent delivery or membership problems to the appropriate email or directory-support team.
