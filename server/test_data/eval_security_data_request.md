# Security Data Request Troubleshooting

## Overview

This article provides guidance for requests involving company, system, account, or security-related data where the requester needs information that may require authorization, approval, or controlled handling.

## Symptoms

Common requests include:

- Request for company security data
- Request for account-related information
- Request for system or access information
- Request for security records or logs
- Request for information that may be restricted or protected

## Identify the Requested Data

Confirm exactly what information the requester is asking for.

Record the purpose of the request and the business context provided by the requester.

## Confirm the Approved Request Process

Determine whether the request must be handled through an approved security, privacy, access-management, or data-request process.

Do not provide restricted information simply because the requester asks for it.

## Verify Authorization

Use the organization's approved process to determine whether the requester is authorized to receive the requested information.

Do not bypass identity or authorization requirements.

## Provide Only Approved Information

Only provide data through the organization's approved process and according to applicable access and data-handling requirements.

Do not disclose credentials, secrets, private security information, or other restricted information as a troubleshooting shortcut.

## Record the Request

Record:

- Requested information
- Requester identity through the approved verification process
- Business purpose when required
- Approval or authorization status
- Any relevant system or security context

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- Authorization cannot be established
- Restricted or sensitive information is requested
- The request requires security or privacy approval
- The applicable data-handling process is unclear
- Administrative action is required

## Important Safety Notes

Do not disclose restricted information without authorization.

Do not provide passwords, authentication secrets, or security credentials.

Do not bypass approved approval or data-request procedures.

## Resolution Outcome

The expected outcome is completion of the request through the appropriate approved process or escalation when authorization or handling requirements cannot be established.

## Escalation

Escalation is recommended when the requested information requires controlled access, approval, or administrative handling.
